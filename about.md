# THE SPIRIT OF NERVOUS EXCITEMENT

that feeling of butterflies in your stomach.

The essence of this being is transformation, something new, something fresh, perhaps even the quality of spring. It embodies euphoria mixed with anxious respect, representing beginnings and endings, anticipation within uncertainty, attraction pared with loss of control, and the energy that arises between an exciting challenge and the decision that follows. 

It has no stable physical form in our world. Its presence can only ever be glimpsed — reflected briefly in the nanostructures of butterfly wings, producing a shimmer of light. Most people, however, do not see it at all. They feel it. A flutter, a tightening, a warmth rising through the abdomen — a sensation it has become its own idiom: to have butterflies. Spiritual traditions trace this encounter to the Solar Plexus Chakra, the energetic seat of personal power, will, and self-becoming.
